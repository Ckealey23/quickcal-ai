QuickCalAI

A Chrome extension that turns typed text, spoken requests, highlighted text, and uploaded documents into Google Calendar events and Google Tasks.

What it does

  • Creates, updates, moves, and deletes events on calendars you own.
  • Creates tasks in your default Google Tasks list.
  • Checks other calendars for overlaps after a successful save.
  • Reads PDF, DOCX, or TXT files you choose and proposes dated items for your review before anything is created.
  • Optionally transcribes a short microphone recording so you can speak a request.

How it works

QuickCalAI runs in Chrome. Natural-language parsing, transcription, and document analysis are handled by a hosted service at https://quickcal-ai.onrender.com. That service uses OpenAI models. Calendar and Tasks writes go directly from the extension to Google APIs after you connect your Google account.

Privacy

See the QuickCalAI Privacy Policy for what is sent to Google, Render, and OpenAI, and what stays in Chrome storage.
